# Naija music slang glossary

Source: hand-written for Vibe DJ (drafted with AI help, **reviewed and approved by a Nigerian listener**, 2026-11-15).
Each entry says what the phrase means and how it should change a music request.
"Hints" use the same scale as the song catalog: energy and valence go from 0 to 1.

## Filler words (no music meaning)

### abeg / biko / pls
Meaning: "please" (abeg is Pidgin, biko is Igbo). Politeness only.
Hints: ignore when building the profile.

### oya
Meaning: "come on / let's go". Signals the person is ready to start something.
Hints: slight push toward energy; no genre meaning.

### omo / chai / ehn / sha / o
Meaning: exclamations and fillers ("wow", "anyway", emphasis).
Hints: ignore.

## Parties and celebrations

### gbedu
Meaning: a banger; loud, danceable party music. "Play gbedu" = play something to dance to.
Hints: energy 0.75-0.95, valence 0.7-0.95, mood energetic or happy, tags party/euphoric. Genres: afrobeats, amapiano, afro-fusion, dancehall.

### owambe
Meaning: a big Yoruba-style party (weddings, birthdays, naming ceremonies) with aso-ebi, spraying money and nonstop dancing.
Hints: energy 0.75-0.9, valence 0.8-0.95, mood energetic/happy, tags party/euphoric/playful. Genres: afrobeats, highlife, amapiano. Older guests also love classic highlife.

### Detty December
Meaning: December festive season, mostly in Lagos: concerts, beach parties, people coming home from abroad.
Hints: energy 0.75-0.95, valence 0.8-0.95, tags party/euphoric. Genres: afrobeats, amapiano, afro-fusion, afro-house.

### jaiye / jaye / chop life
Meaning: to enjoy life to the fullest, often lavishly.
Hints: energy 0.7-0.9, valence 0.8-0.95, tags euphoric/party/confident. Genres: afrobeats, amapiano.

### shayo
Meaning: drinking and partying.
Hints: energy 0.7-0.9, valence 0.7-0.9, tags party/playful. Genres: afrobeats, amapiano, afro-house.

### turn up / groove
Meaning: "turn up" = party hard; "we dey go groove" = going out to have fun.
Hints: energy 0.7-0.9, valence 0.7-0.9, mood energetic, tag party. Genres: afrobeats, amapiano, hip hop, dancehall.

### street / street-pop
Meaning: the gritty Lagos street sound (Olamide, Asake, Shallipopi style): Yoruba/Pidgin bars over hard-hitting percussion.
Hints: energy 0.75-0.9, speechiness higher (0.15-0.35), tags confident/party. Genre: afrobeats.

## Dancing

### whine / whine waist
Meaning: to wind your waist; sensual dancing.
Hints: energy 0.6-0.8, danceability high, tags groovy/romantic. Genres: dancehall, afrobeats, amapiano.

### zanku / legwork / shaku shaku / gyration
Meaning: Nigerian street dance styles and general vigorous dancing.
Hints: energy 0.8-0.95, danceability high, mood energetic, tag party. Genres: afrobeats (street-pop), amapiano.

## Calm, soft and romantic

### vibe / "something to vibe to"
Meaning: relax and enjoy; music that feels good without being loud.
Hints: energy 0.45-0.65, valence 0.55-0.75, mood chill/relaxed, tags groovy/smooth. Genres: afrobeats, afro-fusion, alte, r&b, amapiano.

### low-key / "nothing too loud"
Meaning: calm, quiet, not attention-grabbing.
Hints: lower the energy target by about 0.2 (usually 0.3-0.55). Mood chill or relaxed.

### soft life
Meaning: easy, comfortable, stress-free living (the opposite of struggle).
Hints: energy 0.45-0.65, valence 0.65-0.85, mood relaxed/chill, tags smooth/groovy. Genres: afrobeats, alte, r&b, amapiano.

### Sunday chill / Sunday rice
Meaning: relaxed Sunday afternoon after church, with jollof or fried rice at home.
Hints: energy 0.35-0.6, valence 0.6-0.8, mood relaxed, tags cozy/smooth/nostalgic. Genres: highlife, soulful amapiano, afrobeats, gospel.

### palm wine music
Meaning: gentle guitar-led West African music, like the old palm-wine bars.
Hints: energy 0.35-0.55, acoustic leaning, tags nostalgic/peaceful. Genres: highlife.

### my baby / my person / love matter
Meaning: romance; songs for or about a partner.
Hints: mood romantic, valence 0.55-0.75, energy 0.45-0.65, tags romantic/smooth. Genres: afro-fusion, afrobeats, r&b, alte.

## Low moods

### I dey feel somehow / somehow
Meaning: feeling off, uneasy, a bit down, without a clear reason.
Hints: valence 0.25-0.45, energy 0.35-0.55, mood moody/sad, tags melancholic/dreamy. Genres: afro-fusion (Omah Lay style), alte, r&b, lofi.

### heartbreak / "my babe don japa" / "she don leave me"
Meaning: a breakup (japa = to leave/run away, often abroad).
Hints: valence 0.15-0.4, energy 0.3-0.55, mood sad, tags heartbroken/melancholic. Genres: afro-fusion, r&b, folk, pop.

### sapa
Meaning: being broke, usually at the end of the month.
Hints: wants something uplifting or funny to lift the mood: valence 0.6-0.85, tags hopeful/playful. Genres: afrobeats, highlife, gospel.

### wahala / my head dey hot
Meaning: trouble, stress, frustration.
Hints: two options. If they want to vent: energy 0.8+, mood intense/angry (drill, trap, metal). If they want to calm down: energy 0.3-0.5, mood relaxed. If unclear, choose calming and say so.

## Faith

### praise / praise session
Meaning: upbeat, celebratory church music; people dance.
Hints: energy 0.75-0.9, valence 0.8-0.95, mood happy/energetic, tag uplifting. Genre: gospel.

### worship
Meaning: slow, reflective, prayerful church music.
Hints: energy 0.3-0.55, valence 0.45-0.65, mood relaxed, tags peaceful/hopeful. Genre: gospel.

## Places and moments

### Lagos traffic / go-slow
Meaning: stuck in slow traffic, a long ride.
Hints: energy 0.5-0.7, valence 0.6-0.8, mood relaxed/happy, tags groovy. Genres: afrobeats, afro-fusion, highlife, amapiano.

### beach (Elegushi, Landmark)
Meaning: a Lagos beach day or beach party.
Hints: energy 0.65-0.85, valence 0.75-0.95, tags party/groovy/euphoric. Genres: afrobeats, amapiano, afro-house, dancehall.
