# Activity and moment guide

Source: hand-written for Vibe DJ (drafted with AI help, reviewed and approved by me, 2026-11-15).
Each entry turns an everyday situation into a music profile. Energy, valence, speechiness and
instrumentalness go from 0 to 1, the same scale as the song catalog.

## Focus

### studying / reading / homework
Lyrics pull attention away from words on a page, so instrumental music works best.
Hints: energy 0.25-0.5, valence 0.4-0.65, mood focused/chill, instrumentalness 0.6+, speechiness under 0.1, tags focused/peaceful. Genres: lofi, ambient, classical, jazz.

### coding / deep work
Steady, repetitive grooves help long focus sessions; light vocals are OK.
Hints: energy 0.4-0.65, mood focused/chill, instrumentalness 0.4+, tags focused/dreamy. Genres: lofi, synthwave, liquid dnb, ambient, house.

## Exercise

### gym / weightlifting
Hard-hitting beats and aggressive energy.
Hints: energy 0.8-1.0, mood intense/energetic, tags aggressive/confident. Genres: hip hop, trap, drill, metal, rock, afrobeats (street-pop).

### running / cardio
Steady fast tempo (about 120-170 BPM) that keeps you moving.
Hints: energy 0.75-0.95, valence 0.55-0.85, mood energetic, tags euphoric/confident. Genres: pop, house, liquid dnb, afro-house, amapiano, punk.

### yoga / stretching / meditation
Calm and spacious, little or no singing.
Hints: energy 0.1-0.35, valence 0.4-0.65, mood relaxed, instrumentalness 0.6+, tags peaceful/dreamy. Genres: ambient, classical.

## Rest

### sleep / winding down at night
Very calm, slow and soft.
Hints: energy 0.05-0.3, valence 0.3-0.6, mood relaxed, acoustic leaning, tags peaceful/dreamy. Genres: ambient, classical, lofi.

### rainy day
Cozy and reflective.
Hints: energy 0.25-0.5, valence 0.3-0.55, mood chill/moody, tags cozy/melancholic/nostalgic. Genres: lofi, jazz, folk, r&b, alte.

### morning / waking up
Gentle start that builds up, bright and hopeful.
Hints: energy 0.45-0.7, valence 0.65-0.85, mood happy, tags uplifting/hopeful. Genres: indie pop, soul, afrobeats, folk, gospel.

## Social

### party / club night
Peak-time dance music.
Hints: energy 0.8-0.95, valence 0.7-0.95, mood energetic, danceability high, tags party/euphoric. Genres: afrobeats, amapiano, house, afro-house, reggaeton, dancehall, disco, pop.

### hangout with friends / pre-game / getting ready to go out
Upbeat and fun but not peak intensity; good for talking over.
Hints: energy 0.6-0.8, valence 0.7-0.9, mood happy, tags playful/groovy. Genres: afrobeats, pop, funk, r&b, hip hop.

### date night / romantic dinner
Warm, smooth and intimate.
Hints: energy 0.35-0.6, valence 0.5-0.75, mood romantic, tags romantic/smooth. Genres: r&b, soul, afro-fusion, jazz, alte.

### cooking / cleaning the house
Feel-good grooves to sing along to.
Hints: energy 0.6-0.8, valence 0.75-0.95, mood happy, tags groovy/playful. Genres: funk, disco, afrobeats, highlife, soul, pop.

## Travel

### road trip / long drive
Sing-along songs with a sense of movement.
Hints: energy 0.6-0.8, valence 0.65-0.85, mood happy/energetic, tags uplifting/nostalgic. Genres: rock, pop, country, indie pop, afrobeats.

### late-night drive
Moody, atmospheric and smooth.
Hints: energy 0.45-0.7, valence 0.3-0.55, mood moody/chill, tags dreamy/dark/nostalgic. Genres: synthwave, r&b, alte, hip hop, house.

## Feelings

### heartbreak / after a breakup
Songs that sit with the sadness.
Hints: energy 0.25-0.5, valence 0.1-0.35, mood sad, tags heartbroken/melancholic. Genres: r&b, folk, pop, afro-fusion, soul, country.

### confidence boost / main character energy
Swagger and self-belief.
Hints: energy 0.7-0.9, valence 0.55-0.85, mood energetic, tags confident. Genres: hip hop, trap, pop, afrobeats, k-pop.

### angry / need to let it out
Loud, fast and aggressive.
Hints: energy 0.85-1.0, valence 0.1-0.35, mood angry/intense, tags aggressive/rebellious. Genres: metal, punk, drill, trap, rock.

### nostalgic / throwback
Older songs that bring back memories.
Hints: prefer release decades 1960-2000, tags nostalgic. Genres: soul, funk, disco, highlife, rock, blues.
